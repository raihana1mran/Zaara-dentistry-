India’s top dental hospital

Zaara Dentistry in Madurai has been growing rapidly for over 65 years since 1960, and is now proudly led by the third generation of dentists in Madurai.

We are proud to be recognized as the best dental hospital in India, trusted by lakhs of patients for world-class dental care. With a blend of advanced technology and compassionate service, every patient enjoys a comfortable and stress-free experience.

Our expert team specializes in dental implants, smile corrections, orthodontics, cosmetic dentistry, pediatric dentistry and full mouth rehabilitation. We combine expertise with state-of-the-art equipment to provide Invisalign, smile makeovers, pediatric care and laser dentistry that are precise, painless and comfortable, whether it is a routine check-up or a complex procedure.

BEST DENTIST IN INDIA

Most trusted dentist in India

Dr. Aafaque is a promising Orthodontist and Dentofacial Orthopaedist. He took over the practice from his dentist grandfather, who started practising in 1960.

His motto is “To deliver an international standard of dental practice.” He is one of the top Invisalign providers in India, has international PubMed publications to his credit, and his areas of interest are invisible orthodontics and implants.

He completed his Fellowship in Implantology from ICOI (U.S.A) in 2020 and is Chairman of Zaara Dentistry, where he provides state-of-the-art advanced infrastructure in dentistry.
